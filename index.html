Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us

What is a venture studio and how is it different from a VC?
A venture studio builds companies hands-on (team, product, operations) while VCs primarily fund and advise. We co-create ventures from idea to launch and beyond.
What sectors do you focus on?
We focus on ventures where we can add deep operating value—typically digital products, AI-enabled platforms, fintech, B2B SaaS, and deep-tech when aligned with our capabilities.
What equity stake do you typically take?
It depends on stage and the level of support. We structure fair, transparent terms based on time, capital, and the operational resources committed.
Do you invest in early-stage startups?
Yes—especially at idea to MVP stages where our studio model adds the most value through building, validating, and launching.
How can I apply to partner with Althuraya?
Send us your pitch (problem, solution, market, traction, and team). If it’s a fit, we’ll schedule a short call and share next steps.
